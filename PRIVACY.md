# Sticky Pomodoro privacy policy

Sticky Pomodoro does not collect, send or share any data. It makes no network connections, and it has
no accounts, analytics or tracking. The author receives nothing from your use of it.

It writes only to `%USERPROFILE%\.claude\sticky-pomodoro` on your own computer. That folder holds the
compiled timer and your settings: work and break minutes, window position, and the chime and auto-start
choices. None of it leaves your computer. Delete the folder to remove it.

Questions: https://github.com/griffinb88/sticky-pomodoro/issues
